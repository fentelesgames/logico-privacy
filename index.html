Privacy Policy for Logico

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Fenteles Games respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Logico, including its demo and release versions (the “Game”).

Information We Collect

The Game does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, payment information, or other directly identifying information.

The Android version uses Google Analytics for Firebase. As a result, limited technical and usage information is collected automatically and sent to Google for analytics purposes.

Analytics Data

Google Analytics for Firebase may automatically process:

  • an app-instance identifier, a pseudonymous Logico user identifier, and, where available and permitted by your device settings, an advertising identifier;
  • app lifecycle, session, screen-view, installation, and first-open information;
  • technical information such as device type, operating system, language, app version, and package name;
  • an approximate country or region derived from a masked IP address.

To distinguish a returning player from a genuinely new installation, Logico creates a pseudonymous user identifier on your device. The Game applies a one-way SHA-256 hash to Android's system identifier together with a Logico-specific namespace before sending the result to Firebase. The raw Android system identifier is not transmitted by Logico. The resulting identifier does not contain your name, email address, contact details, or game solutions.

This pseudonymous identifier is used only to reduce duplicate analytics counts and to associate general level events from the same device. On supported Android versions it may remain the same after the Game is uninstalled and installed again on the same device, for the same Android user and app-signing key. It can change after a factory reset, a signing-key change, or changes made by Android.

The Game also sends events indicating when a level is opened or successfully completed for the first time, and when a player later returns to or completes that level again. These events include only the stable level identifier, such as level_1. They do not include the player's name, email address, circuit layout, wires, solution, or other user-created content.

Fenteles Games uses this information to understand installations and general engagement, identify levels that may need improvement, and improve the Game. The Game does not send analytics events for failed checks or measure the amount of time spent solving a level.

Advertising and Crash Reporting

The current version of the Game does not display third-party advertising and does not integrate Firebase Crashlytics or another third-party crash-reporting service.

Fenteles Games does not sell analytics data and does not use the Game's custom level events to create directly identifiable user profiles.

Local Game Data

Your game progress, settings, circuits, placed elements, nodes, and wires are stored locally on your device. The content of these saves is not included in the Firebase Analytics events described above.

Deleting the Game or clearing its local data may permanently remove this locally stored information.

Third-Party Processing

Analytics data is transmitted to and processed by Google LLC through Google Analytics for Firebase. Google may process data on global infrastructure in accordance with its applicable terms and policies. For more information, see Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play may separately process technical information related to downloading, installing, updating, or reviewing the Game under Google's own policies.

Internet Connection

An internet connection is not required for the Game's core gameplay. When a connection is available, Firebase Analytics may transmit the technical and usage information described in this policy.

Data Security, Retention, and Deletion

Analytics data is encrypted in transit. User-level and event-level Analytics data is retained for no longer than 14 months under the available Google Analytics retention settings. Aggregated standard reports may remain available for longer.

The Game does not maintain user accounts or a database that links analytics events to your name or contact details. You can stop future collection from the Game by uninstalling it. Reinstalling the Game may reuse the pseudonymous Logico identifier described above, although no analytics events are sent while the Game is not installed. You can also manage or reset your device's advertising identifier through Android settings, where available.

For privacy or deletion questions, contact Fenteles Games using the address below. Requests will be handled as required by applicable law and where technically possible.

Children's Privacy

The Game does not knowingly request names, contact details, or other directly identifying information from children. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to Fenteles Games, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Game's functionality, third-party services, or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
