Privacy Policy for Logico Demo

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Fenteles Games respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Logico Demo.

Information Collection

Logico Demo does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information.

The game does not require account registration and does not request personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or payment information.

Analytics and Advertising

Logico Demo does not currently use:

  • advertising services;
  • analytics services;
  • tracking technologies;
  • crash-reporting services;
  • personalized advertising;
  • third-party user profiling.

Local Game Data

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to Fenteles Games or any third party.

Deleting the application or clearing its local data may permanently remove your saved progress and settings.

Internet Connection

Logico Demo does not require an internet connection for its core gameplay and does not transmit gameplay information to external servers.

Google Play may independently process technical information related to downloading, installing, updating, or reviewing the application. Such processing is governed by Google's own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Logico Demo does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Since the application does not collect personal information, no personal user profiles are created or maintained.

Third-Party Services

The current version of Logico Demo does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
